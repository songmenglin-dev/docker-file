SequoiaDB 巨杉数据库可以通过 SQL 语句进行数据操作，支持的语句如下：

语句 | 描述 | 示例  
---|---|---  
[create collectionspace](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190951-edition_id-304) | 创建数据库中的集合空间 | db.execUpdate("create collectionspace sample")  
[drop collectionspace](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190952-edition_id-304) | 删除数据库中指定的集合空间 | db.execUpdate("drop collectionspace sample")  
[create collection](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190953-edition_id-304) | 创建集合 | db.execUpdate("create collection sample.employee")  
[drop collection](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190954-edition_id-304) | 删除集合 | db.execUpdate("drop collection sample.employee")  
[create index](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190955-edition_id-304) | 创建索引 | db.execUpdate("create index test_index1 on sample.employee (age)")  
[drop index](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190956-edition_id-304) | 删除索引 | db.execUpdate("drop index test_index on sample.employee")  
[list collectionspaces](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190957-edition_id-304) | 枚举数据库中所有的集合空间 | db.exec("list collectionspaces")  
[list collections](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190958-edition_id-304) | 枚举数据库中所有的集合 | db.exec("list collections")  
[insert into](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190959-edition_id-304) | 向集合中插入数据 | db.execUpdate("insert into sample.employee(age,name) values(25,"Tom")")  
[select](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190960-edition_id-304) | 查询数据 | db.exec( "select age,name from sample.employee" )  
[update](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190961-edition_id-304) | 更新数据 | db.execUpdate( "update sample.employee set age=20" )  
[delete](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190962-edition_id-304) | 删除数据 | db.execUpdate("delete from sample.employee")  
[transaction](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1520813139-edition_id-304) | 事务操作 | db.execUpdate( "begin transaction" )
