SequoiaDB 巨杉数据库中 SQL 语句可以嵌套子句进行数据库操作，支持的子句如下：

语句 | 描述 | 示例  
---|---|---  
[where](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1520813394-edition_id-304) | 过滤记录 | db.exec("select * from sample.employee where age > 10")  
[group by](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190963-edition_id-304) | 对结果集进行分组 | db.exec("select dept_no, count(emp_no) as 员工总数 from sample.employee group by dept_no")  
[order by](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190964-edition_id-304) | 对结果集排序 | db.exec( "select dept_no,count(emp_no) as 员工总数 from sample.employee group by dept_no order by dept_no desc" )  
[split by](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190965-edition_id-304) | 拆分记录 | db.exec( "select a,b,c from sample.employee split by c" )  
[limit](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190966-edition_id-304) | 限制返回的记录个数 | db.exec("select * from sample.employee limit 2")  
[offset](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190967-edition_id-304) | 设置跳过的记录数量 | db.exec("select * from sample.employee offset 3")  
[as](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190968-edition_id-304) | 为集合名、字段名或结果集指定别名 | db.exec("select age as 年龄 from sample.employee where age>10")  
[inner join](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190969-edition_id-304) | 内连接 | db.exec( "select T1.a, T2.b from sample.employee1 as T1 inner join sample.employee2 as T2 on T1.a < 10" )  
[left outer join](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190970-edition_id-304) | 左连接 | db.exec("select t1.LastName, t1.FirstName, t2.OrderNo from sample.persons as t1 left outer join sample.orders as t2 on t1.Id_P=t2.Id_P")  
[right outer join](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1432190971-edition_id-304) | 右链接 | db.exec("select t1.LastName, t1.FirstName, t2.OrderNo from sample.persons as t1 right outer join sample.orders as t2 on t1.Id_P=t2.Id_P")  
[hint](/cn/sequoiadb-cat_id-1445318584-edition_id-304) | 显式地控制执行方式或者执行计划 | db.exec("select * from sample.employee1 where a = 1 /*+use_index(idx_employee1_a)*/")
